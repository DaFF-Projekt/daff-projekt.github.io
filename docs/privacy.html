Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you access the website, GitHub processes the data that are technically necessary to deliver it, in particular your IP address, date and time of access, the requested page and information about your web browser. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor is logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable presentation of the project (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

The data may be processed in the USA. For such transfers GitHub relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which it is certified, and on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. GitHub does not state a fixed retention period for these logs; it keeps data as long as required for the purposes mentioned and for legal obligations. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The operators of this website have no access to these access data.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, web analytics or tracking, and does not embed content from third parties such as fonts, videos or social media plugins. A small script runs only in your browser to position the “Today” marker on the timeline; it neither stores nor transmits any data.

Contact by email

The contact address given on this website is that of the project coordination at Leibniz University Hannover. If you write to it, Leibniz University Hannover processes your email address and your message to answer your inquiry. Its privacy policy applies. If you write to the DFKI, for example to the addresses in the imprint, the DFKI processes your message for the same purpose (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and deletes it once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

Links to other websites

This website contains links to websites of the project partners, funding bodies, publishers and repositories. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, please contact the data protection officer. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

The project “Data Management for Field Research (DaFF)” is funded by the program “zukunft.niedersachsen”, funding line Lower Saxony State Initiative for Research Data Management (FDM-NDS), under the umbrella of Hochschule.digital Niedersachsen. Granting authority: Lower Saxony Ministry of Science and Culture (MWK).
